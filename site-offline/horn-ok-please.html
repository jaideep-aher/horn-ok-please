Limits

  • Do not paint these phrases on a vehicle. This is not a crash risk and not a road test.
  • No local open-weight model passed the STOP versus GO gate. The explanation pilot is six drawings.
  • The photograph pack has no empty lane, so GO recall is undefined.
  • No attention map was drawn. AI coding assistants helped build the tooling. The design decisions, the protocols, and the analysis were reviewed by Jaideep Aher.
