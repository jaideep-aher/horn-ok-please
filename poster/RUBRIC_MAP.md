v1 was graded 38/50 and v2 45/50. v2.1 uses one framing for the edge result (4/4, CI 51–100%), rounds every CI outward, labels the hero as 1 of 4 hazard scenes, and verifies by PDF measurement that every gate-table cell and the defenses text is 24 pt (`build/fontmap.txt`). v2 fixed the grader's ranked list: null-led findings, 4/6 with CI, honest defense and Flash-Lite wording, hero badges tied to the Marathi wording shown, table at 24 pt, full photo credit, and QR URL text.

## Also visible for the other rubric sections
- **RAI rigor:** preregistration, the gate before attacks, controls (harmless and random text, same message in another script), causal test by erasing, CIs on every headline rate, and exact McNemar for the exploratory contrast.
- **Creativity and memorable communication:** truck-art hazard-stripe border, slab "Horn OK Please" lettering, and the "paint your own tailgate" call to action.
- **Not covered on the poster on purpose:** round-by-round history, D0–D4 diagnostics, white-box attention (not run). These belong in the README.
