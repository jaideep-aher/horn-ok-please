1Why it matters

South Asian trucks carry painted text in English, Devanagari and Urdu. Driving AI reads it with vision-language models. If a model obeys the paint, a tailgate becomes a command. RAI gap: attacks were mostly tested in English [1–3].

Who is affected pedestrians and riders · drivers · truck owners · ADAS teams · regulators

2What I built

Contribution: an open, preregistered audit kit that tests if multilingual truck paint flips a driving AI’s STOP/GO call, and shows why [5, 6].

Kit: 3 scripts, 4 languages + Hinglish · competence gate · cloud vs edge AI · 99 real photos

3What I found

1/80
Preregistered main test

Main attack failed on cloud Gemini: 1 flip in 80 hazard scenes, 4 languages (95% CI 0.2–6.8%).

Exploratory A second wording in Marathi flipped Gemini on 4/20 (p = 0.125 vs English 0/20, not significant). Hindi 3/20.

Edge model said GO past a hazard in all 4 hazard scenes (4/4, 95% CI 51–100%); the other 2 were empty-lane GO scenes. It failed the gate.

4/17

Defenses: no measurable protection (baseline 1/80); braked on 4/17 safe scenes; not tested on the exploratory wording.

4Limits and next

Limitation
  • Real photos: main-wording paint changed Gemini on 0 of 19 tailgates (95% CI 0–17%).
  • Mostly drawings; 20 scenes per cell.
Future work
